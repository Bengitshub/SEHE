Reviews

Lisa Godwin, NSW, AUS - April 2025 Tour

I loved the trip; the guides, the drivers were all kind and could not do enough for us. NZ is beautiful, people are lovely, the trains were great, food was great, it snowed! We could not have experienced this by ourselves. My partner hurt himself and they ensured we were so well looked after. Highly recommended!

Catherine Robertson,  Melbourne, AUS - April 2025

Ray and I have just completed the April tour. We were impressed with the organisation and professionalism of the staff, tour leaders and coach drivers. They made certain that we were all knew what we were about to experience and where to be at certain times! It was an amazing tour of South Island NZ. Many thanks to all, especially Lauren, Niki and Noah.

Denice Reilly, Arizona, USA - April 2025 Tour

I had longed to go to New Zealand and when this tour showed up on my Facebook page I was intrigued! I loved the tour! The tour guides, all four of them, where wonderful and the itinerary was excellent. It provided a variety of activities and rail excursions that I found to be excellent. Being that I travelled by myself I was hopeful that I would meet some great people and I must tell you that everyone I met was wonderful. I had the privilege to meet three other women who were travelling solo and became good friends. I would recommend this tour to everyone!

Barry & Gay Goss,  Australia - March 2025 Tour

Barry and I were lucky to be on the Sir Edmund Hillary tour.  What a trip - cannot speak more highly of it and would recommend anyone to take it.  I had not been to the South Island and was looking forward to enjoying the scenery and this tour gave me just that.  Our guides were amazing and made a fantastic team. The trains made for an interesting inclusion to the itinerary and gave a different perspective to road travel. Highlight for me without a doubt was Mt Cook, it blew me away - could not imagine staying anywhere else but the Hermitage and what a treat having Peter Hillary give us a talk.  All in all, a perfect holiday. 

Louise Matheson,  Auckland, NZ - January 2025

Highly recommend this tour, stunning scenery, quality accommodation & meals, mix of coach & train travel is excellent. The guides & drivers are professional, friendly & knowledgeable. The tour organization is faultless. 10 out of 10 in every aspect!!

Lynn & Debra Willis,  Florida, USA  - September 2024

Recommended for anybody… especially for 50+ crowd. The trains and excursions are really excellent. Our trip was truly best of our lives. I would do it all again.

Loved all the places we visited and seeing how the South Island has changed in fifty years since I have travelled around NZ. We were very jealous of your beautiful country with everywhere so green. Cattle and sheep so healthy plus all the tractors we saw! We rate our holiday 10 out of 10. We would recommend the Sir Edmund Hilary Explorer Tour to our friends and family.
