The Seasons

The seasons change everything.

Every journey runs in one of two seasons, and the South Island feels very different in each.

September – April

Spring & Summer

Long golden evenings, turquoise lakes, waterfalls at full song in Milford Sound, and the high country green from the carriage window. Our 11 and 14-day tours run right through the warm months.

June – September

Winter Edition

The Southern Alps under deep snow, crackling fires in high-country hotels, and some of the clearest night skies on Earth over Tekapo. Winter is the South Island’s quieter, cosier season, and our 12-day Winter Editions run right through it.

And one January like no other: the Pinnacle Tour travels with Peter Hillary for a single summer departure — see the Pinnacle Tour.

The “Super 6”

The rail adventures at the heart of it all

The complete set of the South Island’s celebrated rail experiences: scheduled services, heritage steam, and The Mainlander, an exclusive charter no other tour carries.

Which trains ride on which tour? The 14-day Spring/Summer tours include the complete “Super 6”; our 11-day, Winter and Pinnacle journeys each carry their own hand-picked rail set; every itinerary spells it out.

The Name We Travel Under

Named for the greatest explorer of them all

Sir Edmund Hillary epitomised the New Zealand spirit of adventure, and he is our inspiration in planning your experience of a lifetime. This journey carries his name with his family’s blessing — and carries his spirit through the mountains, glaciers and high country he knew best.

You’ll cross the Southern Alps he trained in, stand beneath Aoraki Mt Cook where his climbing life began, and hear the story told where it happened, by the people closest to it.

At Aoraki Mt Cook, a member of the Hillary family personally joins the tour for an evening at the Sir Edmund Hillary Alpine Centre.

“Adventure for the sake of a dream, for the pleasure of searching.”

— Sir Edmund Hillary
