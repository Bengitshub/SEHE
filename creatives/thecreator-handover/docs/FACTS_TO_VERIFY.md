# Facts in the creative copy that need verification before reuse

_Updated 23 Sep 2026 after the PNG recovery was independently checked._

**How to read this list.** These claims are embedded in the ad creatives exactly as written and are preserved unchanged in the delivered renders. Where a figure from another source is cited alongside (the website, the booking system, a Trustpilot data check), it is a **dated observation for comparison only** — it is not a freshly verified replacement value, and where two sources conflict **neither is assumed correct**. Every item needs confirmation with the operator before the creative is reused.
